Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Acadrix is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, institutional email, role, department
  • Usage data: login times, pages accessed, activity logs
  • Academic data: grades, attendance, assignments (role-dependent)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and authorize access to the portal
  • To provide role-specific academic services
  • To improve system performance and user experience
  • To send important system notifications

3. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to personal data is restricted on a need-to-know basis. We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for the duration of your enrollment or employment. You may request deletion of your account by contacting the administrator.

5. Your Rights

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to request deletion
  • Right to data portability

6. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact: admin@college.edu.in
